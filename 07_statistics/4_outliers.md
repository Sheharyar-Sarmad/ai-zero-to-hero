
<!-- Outliers -->

Outliers are data values that are much higher or much lower than the rest of the data. An outlier is a value that is unusually far away from the other values in a dataset.

<!-- Example -->

Data: 10, 12, 13, 15, 16, 18, 100
Here, 100 is much larger than all the other values.
So, 100 is an outlier.

<!-- Upper Fence -->

The Upper Fence is the largest value that is considered normal.

<!-- Formula -->
Upper Fence = Q3 + 1.5 × IQR

<!-- Lower Fence -->

The Lower Fence is the smallest value that is considered normal.

<!-- Formula -->
Lower Fence= Q1 − 1.5 × IQR
