

**Probability Events: Independent vs Dependent vs Mutually Exclusive**

**Independent Events:** One event does NOT affect the other. P(A|B) = P(A) and P(B|A) = P(B). Multiplication: P(A∩B) = P(A)×P(B). Addition: P(A∪B) = P(A)+P(B)-P(A)P(B). Example: Coin flips, rolling dice, drawing cards WITH replacement.

**Dependent Events:** One event AFFECTS the other. P(A|B) ≠ P(A) and P(B|A) ≠ P(B). Multiplication: P(A∩B) = P(A)×P(B|A). Addition: P(A∪B) = P(A)+P(B)-P(A∩B). Example: Drawing cards WITHOUT replacement, weather effects.

**Mutually Exclusive Events:** Events CANNOT occur together. P(A∩B) = 0, P(A|B) = 0, P(B|A) = 0. Addition: P(A∪B) = P(A)+P(B). For 3+ events: P(A∪B∪C) = P(A)+P(B)+P(C). Example: Heads & Tails, Heart & Spade in one draw, rolling 2 & 5 on one die.

**Comparison:** Independent: P(A|B)=P(A), Multiplication: P(A)P(B). Dependent: P(A|B)≠P(A), Multiplication: P(A)P(B|A). ME: P(A∩B)=0, Addition: P(A)+P(B). **Key Rule:** Mutually Exclusive events are ALWAYS dependent. Independent events are NEVER mutually exclusive (unless P(A)=0 or P(B)=0). ME events = no overlap in Venn diagram; Independent = overlap = P(A)P(B); Dependent = overlap ≠ product. Examples: P(Heart then Heart with replacement) = 13/52×13/52 = 1/16 (Independent). P(Heart then Heart no replacement) = 13/52×12/51 = 1/221 (Dependent). P(Heart or Spade) = 13/52+13/52 = 1/2 (ME). P(Heart or Ace) = 13/52+4/52-1/52 = 4/13 (NOT ME because Ace of Hearts exists).
