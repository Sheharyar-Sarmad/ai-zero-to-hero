
<!-- Measure of central tendency -->

Measures of central tendency are methods used to find the central or average value of a dataset.

<!-- Parts -->

The Three Measures of Central Tendency : 

Mean → The average value.
Median → The middle value.
Mode → The most frequently occurring value.

<!-- Mean -->
Mean is the average of a set of data. It is found by adding all the values and dividing the sum by the total number of values. Mean is the average value of a dataset. its represented by x̄(x bar). 

<!-- Formula -->
Mean = Total number of observations / Sum of all observations
	​
<!-- Example -->
Suppose the marks of 5 students are:

40, 50, 60, 70, 80

Step 1: Add all the values
40+50+60+70+80=300

Step 2: Count the observations
There are 5 students.

Step 3: Divide
Mean = 5 / 300 = 60

Answer: The mean marks = 60.

<!-- Median -->
Median is the middle value of a dataset when the data is arranged in ascending or descending order. Median is the middle number in an ordered dataset. Before finding the median, always arrange the data in order (smallest to largest or largest to smallest). There is no single universal symbol for the median like there is for the mean.

<!-- Example -->
Example 1 (Odd Number of Observations)

Data:
10, 15, 20, 25, 30
The middle value is:
10   15   20   25   30
          ↑
       Median = 20

Answer: Median=20
	​
Example 2 (Even Number of Observations)

Data:
10, 20, 30, 40
There are two middle values:
10   20   30   40
     ↑    ↑

Take their average: 20+30 / 2 = 25

So, Median = 25
	​
<!-- Mode -->
Mode is the value that appears most frequently in a dataset. Mode is the number that occurs the most times.

<!-- Example 1 -->
Data:
2, 3, 4, 4, 5, 6, 4, 7

Count the frequencies:

Value	Frequency
2	    1
3	    1
4	    3
5	    1
6	    1
7	    1

Since 4 appears 3 times (more than any other value),

Mode (Mo)=4

<!-- Example 2 (No Mode) -->
Data:
2, 3, 4, 5, 6
Every value appears only once.
So,There is no mode.

<!-- Example 3 (Two Modes) -->
Data:
2, 2, 3, 4, 4, 5
2 appears 2 times
4 appears 2 times

Both have the highest frequency.
So the dataset has two modes.
This is called a bimodal dataset.
Mode=2 and 4
	​
<!-- Example 4 (Three Modes) -->

Data:
1, 1, 2, 2, 3, 3
All three values occur twice.
This dataset has three modes.
It is called trimodal.

Finding the IQR(InterQuartile Range) :

<!-- 1st Problem : -->

data set: 8, 2, 10, 6, 4, 12, 14
sorted data: 2,4,6,8,10,12,14
Upper half: : 2,4,6
Q1 = 4
Lower Half: 10,12,14
Q3 = 12
IQR formula = Q3 - Q1
            = 12 - 4
            = 8 Ans....

<!-- 2nd Problem : -->
