

<!-- 5 number summary -->

A five-number summary is a set of five values that describes the distribution of a dataset by showing its minimum, lower quartile, median, upper quartile, and maximum. A five-number summary consists of five important values that summarize a dataset.

<!-- Important Point -->
Before creating a 5 number summary of a data set we have to remove the outliers first.
When we get the five number summary data then its used to create a box plot

<!-- The Five Numbers -->

1. Minimum (Smallest value)
2. Q1 (First Quartile)
3. Q2 (Median , Half)
4. Q3 (Third Quartile)
5. Maximum (Largest value)

<!-- Example -->

Data: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14

Five-number summary:

Minimum = 2
Q1 = 4
Median (Q2) = 8
Q3 = 12
Maximum = 14
