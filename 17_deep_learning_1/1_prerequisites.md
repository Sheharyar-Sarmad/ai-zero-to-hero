
# Prerequisites Before Deep Learning

## 1. Linear Algebra
- Basics (Vectors, Matrices, Tensors)
- Vector Operations (Addition, Scaling)
- Dot Product & Cross Product
- Linear Transformations
- Linear Equations
- Span & Basis
- Eigenvectors & Eigenvalues

## 2. Statistics
- Basics (Mean, Median, Mode, Variance, Standard Deviation)
- Statistical Visualization (Distributions, Plots)
- Measures of Central Tendency
- Measures of Dispersion
- Probability Distributions (Normal, Bernoulli, etc.)
- T-test, Annova-Test, Z-test, P-test and Mann-Whitney U test
- Correlation & Covariance

## 3. Machine Learning
- Supervised vs Unsupervised Learning
- Train/Test Split & Cross-Validation
- Overfitting & Underfitting
- Bias-Variance Tradeoff
- Key Algorithms (Linear Regression, Logistic Regression, Decision Trees)
- Evaluation Metrics (Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score, Confusion Matrix)

## 4. Calculus (Minimal But Essential)
- Derivatives (Rate of Change)
- Chain Rule (Nested Functions)
- Partial Derivatives (Multiple Variables)
- Gradient Descent (Optimization)
