

## Corpus

---

### What is a Corpus?

> A **corpus** (plural: **corpora**) is a **large collection of text data** used for training and evaluating NLP models.

---

### Key Points

| Aspect            | Explanation |
|--------           |-------------|
| **Definition**    | Large, structured collection of texts |
| **Purpose**       | Train ML models, analyze language patterns |
| **Examples**      | News articles, tweets, books, reviews |
| **Size**          | Can be thousands to billions of words |

---

### Types of Corpus

| Type             | Description            | Example |
|------            |-------------           |---------|
| **Monolingual**  | Single language        | English news articles |
| **Multilingual** | Multiple languages     | English + Hindi tweets |
| **Annotated**    | Labeled data           | Sentiment-labeled reviews |
| **Unannotated**  | Raw text               | Wikipedia dump |

---

### Popular Corpora

| Corpus             | Size                    | Use Case |
|--------            |------                   |----------|
| **Wikipedia**      | Billions of words       | General NLP |
| **Twitter**        | Millions of tweets      | Social media analysis |
| **IMDb Reviews**   | 50k reviews             | Sentiment analysis |
| **News Articles**  | Large                   | Text classification |
| **Book Corpus**    | Massive                 | Language modeling |

---

### Why Corpus Matters?

> More data = Better models

- Learn language patterns
- Train ML models
- Evaluate performance
- Understand language structure

---

### Simple Analogy 

> Corpus is to NLP what a textbook is to a student

- Student learns from textbook → Model learns from corpus

---

### In Short 

**Corpus = Big collection of text**
**↓**
**Used to train NLP models**
**↓**
**Better corpus → Better model**


---

### Quick Summary 

- **Corpus**    =    Large text collection
- **Corpora**   =    Plural form
- **Used for**  =    Training, testing, analyzing
- **Goal**      =    Learn language patterns
- **Bigger**    =    Usually better
