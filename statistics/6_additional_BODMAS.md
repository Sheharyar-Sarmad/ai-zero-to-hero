

<!-- BODMAS  -->

BODMAS is a rule that tells us the order in which mathematical operations should be performed in an expression .Without this rule, the same expression could give different answers.

<!-- BODMAS stands for : --> 

B = Brackets ( ), [ ], { }
O = Orders (Powers/Roots, e.g.)
D = Division (÷)
M = Multiplication (×)
A = Addition (+)
S = Subtraction (−)

<!-- The Concept -->

Imagine you ask two people to solve:
2 + 3 × 4
One person might do:
(2 + 3) × 4 = 20

Another might do:
2 + (3 × 4) = 14

Which is correct?

<!-- BODMAS gives everyone the same rule. -->
Since Multiplication comes before Addition:

2 + 3 × 4
= 2 + 12
= 14

✅ Correct answer = 14

<!-- Example 1 (Brackets First) -->
(2 + 3) × 4

Step 1: Solve the brackets.
5 × 4 = 20

<!-- Example 2 (Orders) -->
2 + 3²

Power first:
2 + 9 = 11

<!-- Example 3 (Division and Multiplication) -->
20 ÷ 5 × 2

Many students think division always comes before multiplication.
❌ That's not the rule.
Division and multiplication have the same priority.
So solve from left to right.

20 ÷ 5 = 4
4 × 2 = 8

✅ Answer = 8

<!-- Example 4 (Addition and Subtraction) -->
10 - 3 + 2

Addition and subtraction also have the same priority.

Solve left to right.
10 - 3 = 7
7 + 2 = 9

✅ Answer = 9
