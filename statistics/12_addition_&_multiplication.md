ADDITION RULE - PROBABILITY OF "A OR B"

General formula for ALL events:
P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A and B)

If events are Mutually Exclusive (cannot happen together):
P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B)
(Because P(A and B) = 0)

For 3 or more ME events:
P(A or B or C) = P(A) + P(B) + P(C)

Examples:
P(Heart or Spade from deck) = 13/52 + 13/52 = 26/52 = 1/2
(Because Heart and Spade are mutually exclusive)

P(Heart or Ace from deck) = 13/52 + 4/52 - 1/52 = 16/52 = 4/13
(Because Ace of Hearts is counted twice, so subtract overlap)

MULTIPLICATION RULE - PROBABILITY OF "A AND B"

For Independent Events (no influence):
P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B)

For Dependent Events (one affects other):
P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B given A)
OR
P(A and B) = P(B) × P(A given B)

Examples:
P(Head then Head in coin flips) = 1/2 × 1/2 = 1/4
(Independent - each flip doesn't affect the next)

P(Ace then Ace without replacement) = 4/52 × 3/51 = 12/2652 = 1/221
(Dependent - first draw changes probability of second)

QUICK SUMMARY:

OR means ADDITION:
P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A and B)
If ME: P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B)

AND means MULTIPLICATION:
P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B given A)
If Independent: P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B)

KEY RULES:
Mutually Exclusive = P(A and B) = 0
Independent = P(B given A) = P(B)

MEMORY TRICK:
OR = Add (subtract overlap if any)
AND = Multiply (use conditional if dependent)
